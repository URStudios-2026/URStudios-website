The Last Signal

About

Venture into the unknown in The Last Signal, a single-player sci-fi survival horror game. Explore the remnants of a lost operation, uncover the fate of a rival corporation, and investigate mysteries that stretch far beyond the edge of known space. Out here, the truth may be more dangerous than the darkness.
