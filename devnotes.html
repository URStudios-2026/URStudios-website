The Last Signal — Development Notes

An ongoing development log for The Last Signal, developed by (Un)ReliAble Studios.*

September 2026

September 12 — Core Gameplay Foundations

- Implemented basic player movement, jumping, sprinting, and mouse controls.

- Added the initial skybox, collision detection, and interaction systems.

September 13 — Gameplay Improvements

- Improved collision detection and player interaction systems.

- Began developing custom 3D assets using Blender.

- Continued refining the sprinting system and addressing gameplay issues.

September 14 — Interaction Systems

- Fixed issues with sprinting and stamina mechanics.

- Improved interaction and button functionality.

- Began development of an interior room featuring an interactive door.

September 15 — Door Mechanics and Environment Blockout

- Implemented door opening and closing functionality with synchronized audio.

- Began grayboxing the primary spacecraft to establish its interior layout.

September 16 — Interior Environment Development

- Expanded the spacecraft's interior layout with additional rooms and doors.

- Implemented button-controlled interior lighting.

- Added visual indicators to buttons to communicate their current states.

September 19 — Interactive Ship Terminal

- Modeled and imported the first ship terminal.

- Implemented terminal collision, player interaction, and user interface functionality.

- Resolved a scripting issue affecting game stability.

September 20 — Ship Systems Interface

- Expanded the ship terminal UI with system power consumption and percentage-based status displays.

- Improved interface formatting and readability.

- Began modeling the spacecraft's pilot seat.

September 24–25 — Pilot Seat Modeling

- Continued development of the pilot seat model.

- Completed the initial seat model and began work on its control panel.

September 26 — Vehicle Controls and Narrative Development

- Continued modeling the pilot seat control panel.

- Added a throttle component to the control assembly.

- Revised the game's narrative direction and began planning additional optional content.

September 27 — New Development Milestones

- Began development of a new gameplay asset.

- Composed the initial version of the game's main theme.

- Continued planning optional content to expand the overall experience.

September 28 — Project Organization

- Reorganized project files and development resources.

- Established a centralized task-tracking spreadsheet to improve development coordination.

October 2026

October 22 — Official Studio Website

- Began development of the official (Un)ReliAble Studios website.

- Established the foundation for sharing project updates and future studio announcements.

*These notes document the game's development over time. Features, mechanics, and plans are subject to change as development continues.*
